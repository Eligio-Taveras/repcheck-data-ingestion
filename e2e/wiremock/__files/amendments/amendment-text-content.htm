                       SENATE AMENDMENT NO. 100

  Mr. SHAHEEN. Mr. President, I rise to offer amendment 100. This
amendment makes the following changes to the bill: it improves the
infrastructure investments by ensuring that funds are targeted to
underserved communities. This is critical to ensuring that the benefits
of this historic legislation reach every American, regardless of zip
code.

  The amendment further requires reporting on disbursement timelines
and provides for an additional review by the Government Accountability
Office to ensure accountability and transparency in the use of funds.

  I urge my colleagues to support this amendment.
