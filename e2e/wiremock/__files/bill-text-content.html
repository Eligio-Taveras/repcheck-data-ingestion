H.R.1 -- E2E Test Healthcare Reform Act (Introduced in House - IH)

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the "E2E Test Healthcare Reform Act".

SECTION 2. FINDINGS.

Congress finds the following:
(1) Healthcare access is a fundamental concern for all Americans.
(2) Reforms to the healthcare system should improve coverage and reduce costs.
(3) Preventive care and early intervention lead to better health outcomes.

SECTION 3. EXPANSION OF COVERAGE.

(a) IN GENERAL.--The Secretary of Health and Human Services shall establish
a program to expand healthcare coverage to underserved communities.

(b) ELIGIBILITY.--To be eligible for the program, an individual must--
(1) be a citizen or lawful permanent resident of the United States;
(2) have a household income that does not exceed 200 percent of the
Federal poverty level; and
(3) not be eligible for coverage under a group health plan.

SECTION 4. FUNDING.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to
carry out this Act for each of fiscal years 2024 through 2029.
